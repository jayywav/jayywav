BOOKING POLICIES

Studio Availability

All bookings are subject to final studio availability. If your requested time is unavailable, you’ll be contacted with the closest available options. If an alternative time can’t be agreed upon, your deposit will be refunded.

Deposits

A $100 deposit is required to reserve your session. Once the studio and session time are confirmed, deposits are non-refundable and are applied toward your remaining session balance.

Rescheduling

Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice to reschedule. Late cancellations and no-shows result in the loss of the deposit.
